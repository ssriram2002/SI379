Hi everyone!! My name is Swetha, I really like harry potter. this game takes you through three trivias.
1) See the character image and guess the character by entering their name
2) Get a quiz of 10 spells and potions to guess. You are given the effect of the spell or potion and then you can guess the name from the 4 given choices. Gold means correct!! Red means wrong :(
3. Get a chapter title and guess which of the 7 books the chapter is in. Click hint if you want the chapter summary as a hint!
Hope you enjoy the game as much as I do!
